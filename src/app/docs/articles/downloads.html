The three formats

PDFpdf
A flat PDF of the redline, best when you are emailing a draft around or printing it out and do not want anyone's local Word settings messing with the layout.

Making a PDF is the only point where a file leaves your computer. redline sends the static redline to its server to render the pages, then deletes it as soon as the PDF comes back.
DOCword · static redline
A .docx where the insertions and deletions are painted onto the text with normal formatting rather than live revision tracking. This is the one to grab if you want to type comments or rewrite a clause right next to the edits without Word stacking tracked changes inside tracked changes until the margin balloons turn into modern art.
DOCword · tracked changes
A standard .docx with real Word revision markup. Open it in Word and use Accept or Reject to work through the changes properly.

Why an option might be greyed out

Before redline lets you download any file, it checks the output against both of your original documents. If something does not match up, that format stays greyed out in the menu so you do not end up sending out a corrupted file. You can see what tripped it in the checks.
