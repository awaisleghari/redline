Drop in two .docx files. In a few seconds, you get a complete forensic breakdown of every word added, deleted, shifted around, or quietly reformatted. No enterprise sales call, no desktop installer from 2009, and no praying that a random web server isn't logging your client's merger agreement.

Your machine, your filesThe diff engine runs entirely inside your browser tab. Your documents never touch a server just to be compared. Paranoid by defaultBefore giving you a download link, redline audits its own output against both source files. If an export fails verification, you never even see it. Free and open sourceNo accounts, no usage caps, no "book a demo" buttons. Read the source code yourself.

How it works

  1. Feed it two drafts.

    Drop in your original .docx and the modified .docx.

  2. Read the damage.

    Inspect every insertion, deletion, moved paragraph, and formatting change right in the browser. Switch between a continuous scroll or paginated Word-style sheets, in light mode or dark mode.

  3. Take your pick of exports.

    Download a PDF, a static Word doc with changes burned into the styling, or a .docx with native Word tracked changes.

Why I built this

I am a lawyer. Half my life is staring at two versions of the same Word document trying to figure out - through existential crises sometimes - why someone did what they did.

If you are not sitting inside a massive firm with an IT department paying Litera-sized licensing fees, your options are bleak: squint at Word's built-in compare tool until your eyes bleed, or upload confidential client files to sketchy online converters. I built redline for my own practice and a few friends who were tired of both. How about you give it a try?

The ground rules

  • Zero AI. Document comparison is a deterministic problem, not a creative writing exercise. An LLM will hallucinate a clause or silently skip a comma. Run the same two files through redline a hundred times and you will get the exact same diff a hundred times.
  • Zero storage. There is no database waiting to leak your drafts. Close the tab and everything vanishes. (The sole exception is PDF rendering, which runs briefly in a sandboxed server pipeline. See privacy for the exact mechanics.)
  • Zero hype. It is a fast, sharp diff tool for .docx files.
