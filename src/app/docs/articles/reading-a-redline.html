The marks

inserted textBlue, with a double underline. It is in the modified version and not in the original.
deleted textRed, struck through. It was in the original and is gone from the modified version.
moved fromGreen, double struck through. Where moved text used to be.
moved toGreen, double underlined. Where it is now. A move counts as one change.
formattingShaded. The words are the same, but bold, italic, underline or similar settings changed.

Moving through the changes

The arrows at the top of the redline jump to the previous or next change, and the counter shows where you are, for example 3 / 10. The current change is outlined so it is easy to find.

Choosing how it looks

  • continuous shows the document as one long page. It is the easiest way to read on a screen.
  • pages shows Word-style pages, so you can find "page 4" when someone refers to it.
  • The moon and sun switch between a dark page and a soft light page. The rest of redline stays dark.
