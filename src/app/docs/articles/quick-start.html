Open the workspace

Click Get started on the home page, or hit open redline in the top bar of these docs. No login screen, no onboarding tour, just two empty dropzones waiting for files.

Drop in the original

Drag the earlier draft into the Original box, or click the box to browse your computer.

Two ground rules: .docx files only (if someone sent you a legacy .doc from 2004, re-save it first), and keep the file under 25 MB.

Drop in the modified version

Put the newer draft into the Modified box.

Double-check that you did not swap the two files, unless you enjoy reading a redline where every addition looks like a deletion and vice versa.

Run the comparison

Click Create redline. Your browser handles the entire diff locally, and even a dense agreement usually wraps up in a few seconds.

Read the damage

Use the arrows at the top to jump straight from edit to edit instead of scrolling through forty pages of boilerplate. You can also flip between a continuous scroll and paginated Word-style sheets, or switch between dark mode and light mode depending on what time of night you are reading this.

Download your copy

Click Download PDF for a locked-down file ready to email, or open the dropdown arrow next to it to grab a Word version (either static formatted markup or native tracked changes).

Every export is automatically audited against your source files before the download option appears. If an output fails verification, redline holds it back rather than handing you a broken file.

What if a file already has tracked changes?

If either draft still has unaccepted redlines floating around inside it, redline treats those changes as accepted before running the comparison, and flags a notice right above your diff so nothing catches you off guard.
