When you compare

Both documents are read and compared inside your browser. They are not uploaded, and nothing about them is sent anywhere.

When you make a PDF

This is the one exception. The static redline is sent to the redline server to be converted into a PDF, and it is deleted there immediately afterwards. Nothing about it is kept. The Word downloads never leave your browser.
